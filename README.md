Project Overview
The NovaTech Solutions website is a small-business website designed to provide customers with information about the company and its technology-related services. The website presents NovaTech Solutions as a professional IT business that helps individuals and businesses with services such as website development, IT support, and computer repairs.
The main purpose of the project is to create a simple, professional, user-friendly, and responsive website that customers can access from different devices, including computers, tablets, and mobile phones.
The website consists of several pages:
Home – Introduces NovaTech Solutions and provides an overview of the business.
About Us – Gives information about the company and its purpose.
Services – Displays the IT services offered by NovaTech Solutions.
Enquiry – Allows potential customers to submit enquiries through an embedded Google Form.
Contact – Provides the company's contact information and location, including a map.
The project was developed using HTML and CSS, with Visual Studio Code used as the development environment. The website also incorporates images, navigation links, an embedded Google Form, and a map to improve the user's experience.
Overall, the project demonstrates how a small business can establish an online presence and provide customers with an easy way to learn about its services, make enquiries, and find its location.

Website goals and objectives
Goal:
The main goal of the NovaTech Solutions website is to create a professional and user-friendly online presence for the business. The website is designed to introduce customers to NovaTech Solutions, showcase its IT services, and make it easy for potential customers to contact the business or submit enquiries.
Objectives:
Provide information about the business – Explain who NovaTech Solutions is, what the business offers, and its purpose.
Showcase IT services – Clearly present services such as website development, IT support, and computer repairs.
Improve customer communication – Provide contact details, a location map, and an enquiry form so customers can easily communicate with the business.
Create a professional online presence – Use a consistent layout, images, navigation, and branding to make the business appear trustworthy and professional.
Ensure easy navigation – Allow users to move between pages such as Home, About Us, Services, Enquiry, and Contact without difficulty.
Support mobile users – Make the website responsive so that it can be viewed comfortably on smartphones, tablets, and computers.
Generate potential customers – Use the enquiry form to allow interested customers to submit their requirements and request further information about the services.

Key Features and Functionality

The NovaTech Solutions website includes several features designed to provide users with information about the business and make it easier for customers to interact with the company.
Navigation Bar – Allows users to easily move between the Home, About Us, Services, Enquiry, and Contact pages.
Home Page – Provides an introduction to NovaTech Solutions and gives users an overview of the business and its IT services.
About Us Page – Provides information about NovaTech Solutions, its purpose, and the type of customers it aims to serve.
Services Page – Displays the main services offered by the business, including website development, IT support, and computer repairs.
Enquiry Form – Allows potential customers to submit enquiries and provide their contact details and requirements. The form is connected to Google Forms for collecting responses.
Contact Page – Provides customers with ways to contact the business and includes a map showing the business location.
Images and Visual Content – Uses business-related images and the NovaTech Solutions logo to make the website more visually appealing and professional.
Responsive Design – The website is designed to adjust to different screen sizes, allowing it to be viewed on computers, tablets, and mobile phones.
Embedded Map – An interactive map is included to help customers locate the business.
Consistent Design – Pages use consistent colours, fonts, navigation, spacing, and branding to provide a unified user experience.
